Frequently Asked Questions

What details are required for a Kundli reading?+

We need your full name, exact date of birth, time of birth (as precise as possible), and place of birth (city, state and country). These details let Panditji prepare an accurate birth chart.

Do you offer online video consultations for outstation clients?+

Yes. Clients outside Nagpur, or outside India, can book an online call. Timing is kept flexible for outstation and international clients — just message on WhatsApp to fix a convenient slot.

Is my confidential information kept private?+

Yes, all personal and birth details shared for a consultation are kept strictly confidential and used only to prepare your reading.

Can I get an in-person consultation at the temple?+

Yes, in-person sessions are available at the office near Dr. Changani, Tekdi Road, Sitabuldi, Nagpur, associated with the Puratan Jagrut Shani Mandir. Select "In-Person Session" when booking.
